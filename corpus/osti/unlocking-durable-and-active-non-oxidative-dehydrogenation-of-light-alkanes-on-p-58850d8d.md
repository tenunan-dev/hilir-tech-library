# Unlocking durable and active non-oxidative dehydrogenation of light alkanes on protonic ceramic electrochemical cells

**Authors:** Xu, Yangsen; Sasaki, Kotaro (ORCID:0000000324748323); Zhang, Xirui; Zhu, Feng; He, Fan; Hu, Feng; Liu, Ying; Choi, YongMan; Chen, Yu

**Abstract:** Breaking the intrinsic activity-durability trade-off remains a major challenge in electrochemical hydrocarbon \\r\\nconversion. Herein, we report a highly active and durable layered perovskite PrBaMn1.8(Ni0.5Mo0.5)0.2O6-δ \\r\\n(PBMNM55) as an efficient electrode for ethane dehydrogenation in protonic ceramic electrochemical cells \\r\\n(PCECs). PBMNM55 demonstrates outstanding electrocatalytic performance, achieving an area-specific resis\\r\\ntance of 4.06 Ω cm2 in 10% C2H6-90% Ar at 700 ◦C. In single-cell operation at 1.0 V and 700 ◦C, the cell delivers \\r\\n42.0% ethane conversion, 85.0% ethylene selectivity, and a hydrogen generation rate of 4.01 mL min 1 cm2 at \\r\\nthe negative electrode. Coupling ethane dehydrogenation with CO2 reduction further enhances the ethane \\r\\nconversion to 52.5%, while yielding a CO production rate of 0.74 mL min 1 cm2. In-situ Raman spectroscopy \\r\\nconfirms excellent resistance to carbon deposition, highlighting the electrode’s robust durability. Density func\\r\\ntional theory (DFT) calculations reveal the intrinsic influence of substitutional Ni-Mo co-doping on the precursor \\r\\nperovskite lattice, including a lower calculated C-H activation barrier before Ni exsolution.
