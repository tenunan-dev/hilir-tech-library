Item 
 
Existing and planned hydrogen liquefaction capacity in the United States total 
approximately 794 mtpd (metric tons per day) as of May 2024. Current total operating 
capacity is estimated a s 304 mtpd from 14  plants with capacities of 5– 32 mtpd. 
Additional capacity from 10 –90 mtpd plants could total 490 mtpd, with 4 plants under 
construction and 9 plants in varying stages of planning and development.  DOE 
continues to monitor projects that are in early development.  Plant locations 1 are 
illustrated in Figure 1. As installations and announcements are continuously being 
updated, this Record only documents information available at the time of this posting 
(May 2024).
